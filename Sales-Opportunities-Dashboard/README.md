
# Sales & Opportunities Analysis Dashboard

## Business Problem

Sales teams often lack visibility into opportunity pipeline stages and revenue contribution, making it difficult to track performance and identify bottlenecks.

## Solution

Built an interactive Power BI dashboard to analyze sales performance, track opportunity stages, and monitor revenue trends for better decision-making.

## Features

* Stage-wise opportunity distribution
* Revenue by producer analysis
* Monthly revenue trend visualization
* KPI tracking (Total Revenue, Opportunities, Closed Won, Exceeded Revenue)
* Dynamic filtering (Stage, Producer, Date)

## Tools

Power BI, DAX, Power Query, Data Modeling

## Key Insights

* Closed Won stage contributes the highest revenue share (~46%)
* Significant dependency on top-performing producer
* Revenue shows mid-year decline indicating potential seasonality
* Early-stage opportunities highlight future pipeline potential
